# Ingestion queue

Works worth ingesting, not yet ingested — each line names the work
and what it would feed. Ingesting removes the line. Identifiers here
are pointers, not bibliography: ingest-paper verifies every
identifier against the registrar regardless.

- Petersen, Vakkalanka & Kuzniarz 2015, "Guidelines for conducting
  systematic mapping studies in software engineering: An update"
  (IST; doi:10.1016/j.infsof.2015.03.007) — the mapping canon's
  update, long a named queue item on [[survey-methodology]]; feeds
  that page and the run-survey skill.
- Kitchenham, Madeyski & Budgen 2023, "SEGRESS: Software Engineering
  Guidelines for REporting Secondary Studies" (TSE;
  doi:10.1109/TSE.2022.3174092) — the SE reporting standard the
  dse method follows; feeds [[survey-methodology]].
- Elliott et al. 2014, "Living Systematic Reviews: An Emerging
  Opportunity to Narrow the Evidence-Practice Gap" (PLoS Medicine;
  identifier at ingest) — the established term our "updatable
  systematic map" crosswalks to; feeds [[survey-methodology]].
- Guyatt et al. 2008, "GRADE: an emerging consensus on rating
  quality of evidence and strength of recommendations" (BMJ;
  identifier at ingest) — source of the Certainty grading adopted
  into evidence records; feeds [[survey-methodology]].
- Toulmin, "The Uses of Argument" (Cambridge UP, 1958/2003 updated
  ed.; book) — the claim/grounds/warrant/qualifier model behind the
  claims and evidence ledgers; feeds [[research-craft]] or a future
  argumentation page.
- Bloomfield & Bishop, "Safety and Assurance Cases: Past, Present
  and Possible Future — an Adelard Perspective" (SSS 2010;
  identifier at ingest) — Claims-Argument-Evidence, the closest
  structural ancestor of the evidence binding layer.
- Kelly & Weaver 2004, "The Goal Structuring Notation — A Safety
  Argument Notation" (identifier at ingest) — GSN, same assurance
  family.
- Clark, Ciccarese & Goble 2014, "Micropublications: a semantic
  model for claims, evidence, arguments and annotations in
  biomedical communications" (J Biomed Semantics;
  doi:10.1186/2041-1480-5-28) — formal claim–evidence networks;
  the semantic-publishing ancestor of claims.md + evidence.md.
- Groth, Gibson & Velterop 2010, "The anatomy of a nanopublication"
  (Information Services & Use; identifier at ingest) — single
  assertions with attached provenance; the statement-level
  counterpart.
