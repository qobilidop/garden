# Open research questions

- **Q01:** Is an active-choice configuration a new semantic object or a standard
  partial path/projected model under another representation?
- **Q02:** Is the graph best modeled as a well-founded term graph, an expression
  DAG, a circuit, or a pure SSA/dataflow function?
- **Q03:** Does “demand” denote operational laziness, semantic dependence, or a
  chosen observation policy over an eager pure graph?
- **Q04:** Must all primitive operators be total? If not, which observations from
  inactive alternatives remain semantically relevant?
- **Q05:** Should equal-valued choice alternatives remain observationally
  distinct, and what user-facing purpose justifies that observation policy?
- **Q06:** Which solver guarantees are necessary for exactness and termination?
- **Q07:** Is the desired algorithm more naturally described as symbolic
  execution, symbolic evaluation, residualization, or projected enumeration?
- **Q08:** Which complexity parameters reveal genuine savings beyond the
  unavoidable output size?
- **Q09:** Is the general theory strong enough for PLDI, or principally a CAV
  method whose value depends on implementation and evaluation?
