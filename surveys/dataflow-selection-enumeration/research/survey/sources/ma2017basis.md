- **Status:** primary-screened; exact state-reduction comparator

- **Version inspected:** primary institutional abstract and author publication

## Evidence locations

- Abstract and problem setup: partition transitions into explicit and implicit
  sets whose implicit subnet is acyclic.
- Primary abstract: abstract implicit firing so a subset of basis markings
  completely characterizes the reachable marking set.
- Primary abstract: maximum-cardinality partitioning is NP-hard, while a
  maximum-set partition is polynomial-time computable.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The primary abstract states an exact compressed representation of state
reachability, including an extension to unbounded nets. It is not an input
partition, and no full-paper detail is transferred beyond that evidence.
