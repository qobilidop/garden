# jonssonkok1989comparison — Comparing fully abstract dataflow models

- **Status:** screened; full-abstraction equivalence boundary
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-51285-3_43
- **Primary author bibliography:** https://user.it.uu.se/~bengt/publications-chron.html
- **Version read:** publisher abstract and primary bibliographic record
- **Bibliography key:** `jonssonkok1989comparison`

## Evidence locations

- Publisher abstract: both compared models are compositional and fully
  abstract for nondeterministic FIFO dataflow networks.
- Publisher abstract: the paper relates the two models and proves them
  isomorphic.
- Closing abstract statement: the models are compared with other compositional
  models that are not fully abstract.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The isomorphism reconciles two whole-network semantic models already present in
the survey. It does not define a finite requested-site observer or map network
traces back to exact symbolic caller-input fibers.
