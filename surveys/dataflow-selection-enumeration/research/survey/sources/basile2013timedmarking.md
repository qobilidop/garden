# basile2013timedmarking — Timed marking estimation with silent transitions

- **Status:** screened; timed inverse-observation antecedent
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ETFA.2013.6648063
- **Version read:** publisher and author abstract plus later correction context
- **Bibliography key:** `basile2013timedmarking`

## Evidence locations

- Abstract: a modified state-class graph and linear programs estimate markings
  consistent with timed observations in the presence of silent transitions.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This predates later observed state-class-graph estimators. Later literature
reports corrections to the original construction, so no unqualified
completeness theorem is imported from abstract-level evidence.
