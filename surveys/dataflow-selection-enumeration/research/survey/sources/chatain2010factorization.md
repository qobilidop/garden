# chatain2010factorization — Symbolic colored-net unfoldings

- **Status:** deep-read; partial-order and composition comparator
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13675-7_11
- **Version read:** author-hosted paper, pp. 165--184
- **Bibliography key:** `chatain2010factorization`

## Evidence locations

- Introduction: unfoldings represent true concurrency without constructing
  every interleaving of concurrent actions.
- Section 4, Proposition 2 and Theorem 4: construct symbolic unfoldings and
  establish the coreflection with symbolic occurrence nets.
- Factorization development: symbolic unfolding of a product is the product of
  compatible component unfoldings.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The work supplies exact omission of redundant interleavings and a formal
composition law for colored-net processes. Its objects are concurrent runs and
interfaces, not requested-root observations and input residuals of a pure DAG.
