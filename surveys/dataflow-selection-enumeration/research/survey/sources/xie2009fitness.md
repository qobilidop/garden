## Evidence locations

- Sections 2--3: define state-dependent fitness functions measuring progress
  toward a requested coverage target and use them to schedule path flips.
- Sections 4--5: implementation and budgeted empirical evaluation.

## Relationship and evidence limit

Fitness changes search order and can focus on one requested target, but does
not establish semantic equivalence, exact pruning, or exhaustive observer
enumeration within a finite budget.
