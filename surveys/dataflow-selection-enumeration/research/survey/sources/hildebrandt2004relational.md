  earlier relational semantics for indeterminate dataflow.

  dataflow bisimulation with a congruence result, and treats higher-order
  dataflow through a biproduct.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The semantic equivalence and compositional structure concern nondeterministic
network processes. They do not define a selected internal observer and compute
all of its exact caller-input inverse fibers.
