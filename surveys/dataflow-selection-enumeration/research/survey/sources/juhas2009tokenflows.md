## Evidence locations

- Abstract and introductory framework: token flows are labelled directed
  acyclic graphs, considered up to isomorphism, whose arcs record token use
  between transition occurrences.
- Definitions 8--21: different interpretations recover process, partial-word,
  rewrite-term, step-sequence, and firing-sequence semantics.
- Conclusion: the framework also supports partial-word acceptance, synthesis
  from partial languages, and token-flow unfolding results.

## Relationship and evidence limit

Token flows unify intensional Petri-net behaviors, including causality and
concurrency. They do not partition an external pure-program input domain or
attach a typed residual computation to each requested observation.
