# aguirre2008observability — Partial-observation reachability estimates

- **Status:** primary-screened; state-estimation observer comparator
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ETFA.2008.4638383
- **Version inspected:** primary ETFA 2008 paper and abstract, pp. 129--136
- **Bibliography key:** `aguirre2008observability`

## Evidence locations

- Abstract and construction: under event-detectability and known-resource
  assumptions, build an estimation reachability graph from partial state
  observations.
- Analysis: use the graph for observability, convergence, and observer design.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This is an explicit partial observer over possible Petri-net states. It solves
state estimation rather than enumerating exact preimages of a total finite
program observer.
