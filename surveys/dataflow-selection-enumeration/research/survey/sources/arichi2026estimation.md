# arichi2026estimation — Estimation without observed transitions

- **Status:** screened; alternate partial-observation model
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10626-026-00450-z
- **Version read:** Springer publisher abstract
- **Bibliography key:** `arichi2026estimation`

## Evidence locations

- Publisher abstract: observable place markings are used first to infer some
  or all firing-vector coordinates. If all transitions are detected, the full
  marking is estimated without the initial marking; otherwise an asymptotic
  observer combines detected transitions, observable markings, and the initial
  marking to estimate the remaining quantities.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This inverts a different observer—place markings when transition firings are
initially all unobserved. Only abstract-level claims are used, and the
asymptotic observer is not an exact caller-input partition.
