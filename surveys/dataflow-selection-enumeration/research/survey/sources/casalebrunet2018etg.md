## Evidence locations

- Primary abstract: an execution trace graph is a DAG whose nodes are action
  firings and whose arcs encode execution constraints between firings.
- Completeness statement: the paper specifies the dependencies and processing
  needed for one ETG to represent all admissible trajectories of a dynamic
  dataflow process-network program.
- Applications: architecture-independent executions annotate implementation
  characteristics for design-space exploration, reduction, and optimization.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This journal article materially strengthens the earlier serial-trace and
guard-dependency lineage with an explicit complete-trajectory claim. Its
enumerated object is a schedule/action-firing trace space. It does not derive
the exact inverse fibers of a sparse requested-site observer over caller input
values, nor attach a typed program residual to each fiber.
