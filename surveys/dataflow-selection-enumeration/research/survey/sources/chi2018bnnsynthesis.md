# chi2018bnnsynthesis — Logic synthesis of binarized networks

- **Status:** primary-screened; exact circuit-compilation neighbor
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3240765.3240822
- **Version inspected:** primary ICCAD paper and abstract, pp. 1--7
- **Bibliography key:** `chi2018bnnsynthesis`

## Evidence locations

- Method: translates binarized neural inference into a logic network and uses
  logic-synthesis operations to optimize implementation.
- Evaluation: compares synthesized circuits on neural benchmarks.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The transformation is an exact extensional hardware representation of BNN
inference, but does not enumerate output classes, observation fibers, or
contextual hidden-event identities.
