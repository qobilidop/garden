## Evidence locations

- Abstract: the work compares variants of indeterminate fair merge in static
  dataflow and proves three inequivalent forms of unbounded indeterminacy.
- Abstract: the proof uses trace semantics and behavior properties invariant
  under network composition.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The quotienting question is the expressive power of network primitives under
composition. It is not a complete finite partition of caller inputs under a
requested sparse program observation.
