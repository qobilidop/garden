# yu2015dynamicpnslicing — Structural-dependency dynamic Petri-net slicing

- **Status:** screened; predecessor to maximal/minimal Petri slicing
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1002/ASJC.1031
- **Version read:** publisher metadata and abstract
- **Bibliography key:** `yu2015dynamicpnslicing`

## Evidence locations

- Abstract: backward analysis constructs a structural dependency graph from a
  slicing criterion.
- Dynamic step: the structural graph is combined with a specified marking to
  obtain a marking-sensitive Petri-net slice.
- Evaluation: the slice supports system analysis on a reduced net.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The work establishes the structural-dependency and marking-directed lineage
that precedes exact maximal/minimal contribution slices. Its accessible
evidence does not establish a complete caller-input partition, exact sparse
observation image, or general residual semantics.
