# corona2003observers — Nondeterministic labeled-net observers

- **Status:** deep-read; exact current-marking observer
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ETFA.2003.1247722
- **Version read:** author-hosted ETFA 2003 full text, pp. 307--314
- **Bibliography key:** `corona2003observers`

## Evidence locations

- Model assumptions: known initial marking and net structure, observed
  transition labels, contact-free nondeterministic equal-label transitions.
- Main construction: represent exactly the current markings consistent with an
  observed word by a fixed-structure linear system independent of word length.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The work is a same-year companion to the Giua--Júlvez--Seatzu estimator and
makes the nondeterministic-label scope explicit. It estimates hidden markings,
not initial caller inputs or typed residual program values.
