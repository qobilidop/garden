# delpeuch2020faceted — Complete language for faceted dataflow

- **Status:** screened; categorical workflow-language boundary
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.4204/EPTCS.323.1
- **Open author copy:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05937
- **Version read:** complete open paper
- **Bibliography key:** `delpeuch2020faceted`

## Evidence locations

- Introduction and Section 2: OpenRefine facets route records according to
  their values and require more structure than an ordinary workflow DAG.
- Sections 3--4: two nested monoidal categories give a three-dimensional
  diagrammatic language for faceted workflows.
- Section 4 and Appendix A: the axioms are complete for the presented model and
  support equational reasoning about workflow equivalence.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The completeness claim concerns a categorical language for ETL workflows and
facet-dependent routing. It is not completeness of feasible program
observations, and it supplies neither inverse caller-input regions nor residuals.
