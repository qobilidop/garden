# giua2003marking — Marking estimation under partial observation

- **Status:** deep-read; direct observation-fiber comparator
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ACC.2003.1238961
- **Version read:** primary full text, pp. 326--331
- **Bibliography key:** `giua2003marking`

## Evidence locations

- Estimator: recursively characterize every marking consistent with an
  observed transition-label word, including nondeterministic equal labels.
- Representation: use a fixed-structure linear system whose form is independent
  of the observation-word length.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This is an exact inverse-consistency set for a requested event observation and
is exceptionally close semantically. It estimates reachable Petri-net markings
after a word, not caller inputs of a pure dataflow function, and has no typed
residual program value.
