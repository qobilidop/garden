## Required adversarial conclusion

Before theory drafting begins, this document must state the strongest known
encoding of our proposed problem into each closest framework and identify which
claimed result, if any, is not inherited from prior work.

## Preliminary adversarial conclusion

The following broad claim is already known and must not be presented as novel:

> Symbolically evaluate a finite program and represent its behavior by disjoint,
> exhaustive guards paired with residual symbolic values.

The partial-map observation shape is also prior art. Braßel and Huch's
branching information is already a lazily extended partial function from
stable shared choice references to positions. Braßel and Fischer then give a
literal list-backed ID-to-decision assignment extended by `hnf`; Braßel's
dissertation supplies value-set adequacy and a concrete `Map ID Choice`
implementation. Set-function fingerprints record only executed
nondeterministic steps; pull-tab fingerprints are finite choice-ID/outcome
maps; memoized pull-tabbing populates a task's map only as choices reach the
root. Demand-driven omission, stable choice identity, sharing, fair or
complete result search, and sparse fingerprints therefore cannot be claimed
as new.

The surviving graph-specific object is narrower: for a fixed deterministic
pure graph and requested roots, observe the outcomes of exactly those
input-selected sites in the enabled closure, retaining observed equal-valued
outcomes and omitting unobserved sites. The desired result is the exact inverse
image of that map over symbolic inputs, paired with a residual value. Ghost
inactive/outcome instrumentation followed by projected AllSMT is an immediate
reduction; output-directed symbolic execution can enumerate the same observer.

The formal work still requiring proof is a commuting correspondence among
enabled closure, guarded fingerprints, ghost logging, concolic residualization,
and activation projection under DAG sharing and contextual site identity.
Its elementary fiber partition and \(K+1\) model-query count are not novelty.
Absent a non-tautological minimality, representation-separation, or reuse
theorem, this is a survey synthesis rather than a PLDI-level new enumeration
paradigm.
