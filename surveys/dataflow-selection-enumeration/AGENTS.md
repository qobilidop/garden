# Project guidance

- Treat `research/survey/sources/` as the evidence record for individual works,
  `research/survey/syntheses/` as the current cross-paper understanding,
  `research/claims.md` as the contribution ledger, and `research/decisions/` as
  the record of durable research choices.
- Do not present a claim as novel merely because no contradicting paper has yet
  been found. Record novelty as a hypothesis until the closest-work audit is
  complete.
- Base literature claims on primary sources. Record a stable URL or DOI and
  pinpoint sections, definitions, theorems, algorithms, or pages for important
  evidence. Keep quotations short and distinguish authors' claims from our
  interpretation.
- Keep durable files synthetic and current rather than chronological. Use Git
  for history; distill scratch notes instead of committing research diaries.
- Do not commit downloaded papers, search-engine exports, build products, or
  contributor-local agent state.
- Run development and verification commands through `./dev.sh`.
- A Codex-authored commit must include:
  `Co-Authored-By: Codex GPT-5.6 Sol <codex@openai.com>`.
