## Evidence used

SEGRESS provides reporting guidance for software-engineering secondary
studies. The manuscript uses it only to motivate an auditable account of
scope, search, screening, extraction, and validity threats.

## Evidence locations

- Guideline construction and reporting framework: items for transparent
  secondary-study reporting.
- Validity and evaluation discussion: limitations of checklists and the need
  to expose study decisions.
