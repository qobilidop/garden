# joyce2025skip — Computing Linear Regions in Neural Networks with Skip Connections

- **Status:** deep-read
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-09645-6_10
- **Bibliography key:** `joyce2025skip`

## Relationship

This proceedings work extends exact activation-vector, region-inequality, and
affine-map recurrences to networks with skip connections. It reinforces that
skip-connection support and exact per-region residual maps are not available
novelty separators. The artifact remains a dense neural activation partition,
not a requested-root-relative observation over arbitrary typed graph sites.
