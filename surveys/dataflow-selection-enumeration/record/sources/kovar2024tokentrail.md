## Evidence locations

- Abstract: the net language is a set of labelled nets intended to represent
  conflict and concurrency without committing to state or partial-language
  expansion.
- Abstract: token-trail semantics covers synchronous net morphisms and the net
  language includes all finite unfoldings.
- Abstract: the paper proves state-transition simulation and a step-language
  union theorem, then gives an inclusion-decision algorithm and implementation.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The construction is a rich whole-Petri-net language semantics. Its completeness
and inclusion results are not an enumeration of requested program-site outcomes
over caller inputs and carry no typed functional residual.
