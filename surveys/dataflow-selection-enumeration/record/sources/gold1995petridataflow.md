# gold1995petridataflow — Compositional dataflow semantics for Petri nets

- **Status:** screened; cross-model dataflow-semantics boundary
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01186644
- **Version read:** publisher abstract and bibliographic record
- **Bibliography key:** `gold1995petridataflow`

## Evidence locations

- Abstract: the semantics describes place/transition-net input-output behavior
  using fixed points and is compositional for parallel composition, feedback,
  and output merging.
- Abstract: consistency with step-sequence semantics supplies an equivalent
  operational semantics.
- Bibliographic record: *Acta Informatica* 32, pp. 627--645.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The result composes nets and preserves their input-output behavior. It does not
enumerate the inverse caller-input sets of a requested internal selection event
or attach typed symbolic residuals to those sets.
