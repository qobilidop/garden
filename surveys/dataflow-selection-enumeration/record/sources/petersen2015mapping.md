# petersen2015mapping — systematic mapping guidance

- **Status:** methodology source
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2015.03.007
- **Bibliography key:** `petersen2015mapping`

## Evidence used

The paper updates guidelines for conducting systematic mapping studies in
software engineering. The manuscript uses it only to characterize the study as
a systematic map organized around research questions, classification, search,
screening, and reporting; no technical claim about selection observations is
attributed to it.

## Evidence locations

- Abstract and method overview: purpose and structure of systematic mapping.
- Reporting guidance: traceable research questions, search, selection, and
  classification procedures.
