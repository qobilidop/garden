# Nondeterministic labeled-net observers

## Evidence

### Evidence locations

- Model assumptions: known initial marking and net structure, observed
  transition labels, contact-free nondeterministic equal-label transitions.
- Main construction: represent exactly the current markings consistent with an
  observed word by a fixed-structure linear system independent of word length.

## Bearing on RQs

The work is a same-year companion to the Giua--Júlvez--Seatzu estimator and
makes the nondeterministic-label scope explicit.

## Evidence limits

- It estimates hidden markings, not initial caller inputs or typed residual
  program values.
