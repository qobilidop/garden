## Evidence locations

- Construction: path constraints are converted to entry objects containing
  exactly the properties required for a selected ArkTS path, and the resulting
  concrete test replays that path.

## Relationship and evidence limit

This directly establishes path-relative sparse input objects. It handles one
chosen path at a time and does not enumerate a duplicate-free observer image
or exact inverse guard for every result.
