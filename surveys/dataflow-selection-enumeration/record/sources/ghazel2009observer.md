## Evidence locations

- Construction: combine temporal constraints with a class graph to infer
  possible current state and event scenarios despite unobservable events.
- Application: online diagnosis from observed events and time information.

## Relationship and evidence limit

The observer explicitly computes hidden-state possibilities from partial
events. It is a timed state estimator rather than a duplicate-free partition
of initial caller inputs with symbolic residuals.
