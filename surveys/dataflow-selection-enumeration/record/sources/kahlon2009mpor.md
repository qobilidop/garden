# kahlon2009mpor — Monotonic partial-order reduction

- **Status:** deep-read; exact symbolic-POR comparator
- **Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-02658-4_31
- **Version read:** CAV 2009 full text
- **Bibliography key:** `kahlon2009mpor`

## Evidence locations

- Algorithm: SAT/SMT constraints restrict executions to quasi-monotonic
  thread-identifier sequences.
- Results: the reduced search is sound and optimal in the declared sense of
  retaining necessary and no redundant interleavings.

## Relationship and evidence limit

MPOR is a stronger symbolic schedule-quotient precedent than generic POR, but
its observations concern concurrent interleavings rather than selected graph
sites and their input preimages.
