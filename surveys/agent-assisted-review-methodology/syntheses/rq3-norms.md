# RQ3 — Norms converge on content, fragment on instrument, and lag in practice

## Convergent content across every guidance source

From the pre-LLM guidance (hamel2021) through the 2024–2026 wave
(gartlehner2025/RAISE, holst2025, degen2024, fernandes2026), the same
core obligations recur: name the tool and version; disclose the exact
prompt/configuration; state which stage and task the AI performed and
what the human did; describe verification; keep a human accountable
(no AI authorship — explicit in gartlehner2025 and fernandes2026); and
never fully automate any stage (gartlehner2025's principle 1; hamel2021
flags autonomous include/exclude as "inappropriate use"; fernandes2026
keeps a human decision-maker in all 9 stages). The sanctioned *positive*
role is consistent too: AI as a secondary, quality-assurance reviewer —
re-checking single-reviewer exclusions and extractions (gartlehner2025),
auditing already-excluded records (hamel2021's AI-audit step).

## The instruments are fragmenting

Four distinct disclosure instruments now compete, none validated:
PRISMA-trAIce's 14 checklist items + a flow diagram splitting human vs
AI exclusions (holst2025 — no pilot, no user study, single institution);
FRAISR's per-stage tool/version/parameters table with machine-readable
CSV (degen2024 — cross-disciplinary framing, no pilot); HAICO-SLR's
dual conduct+reporting tables from a 124-paper review (fernandes2026 —
preliminary, SSRN); and the position-statement layer (RAISE, the
Cochrane RRMG statement, the joint Cochrane/Campbell/JBI/CEE statement).
Meanwhile the officially announced PRISMA-AI addresses reviews *about*
AI and remains unpublished (per holst2025), and luo2024 independently
documents the gap the proposals rush to fill: PRISMA 2020 covers
automation only at the selection items. The map's guideline facet (36
works) suggests more instruments are arriving faster than any is
accumulating adoption evidence — a standards race.

## Practice lags the norms

The field's own coordination body reports that very few published SRs
disclose advanced AI use (oconnor2024/ICASR, citing Wellcome-funded
mapping work), and no body yet enforces evaluation or disclosure
standards. The best available in-practice template is mughal2026: name
the model in the acknowledgments, cite the specific PRISMA item, publish
a validation table (κ, recall, false-omission rate) beside the flow
diagram, and revisit the automation's residual risk in limitations.

## For the sys0 survey layer

The convergent-content list is directly adoptable now (this campaign's
baseline.md discloses model/version/prompts/pass-structure per stage);
instrument choice can wait for the race to settle. The deeper import is
gartlehner2025's sanctioned role inverted: in sys0 the *agent* is the
primary reviewer and the human is the gate — the guidance's asymmetry
(AI checks human) has no published norms for its mirror image yet.
