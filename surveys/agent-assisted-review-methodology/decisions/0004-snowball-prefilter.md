- Status: accepted (agent judgment, 2026-08-08)

2020+ neighbors; compact scale cannot dual-screen them all. Candidates
are kept only when the title matches review-side vocabulary
(review/screening/synthesis/extraction/meta-analysis/survey/

snowballing exists to escape terminology (wohlin2014), and a title

review-side vocabulary rather than the fast-moving AI-side, and
bounded to this round. A v2 baseline can drop the filter.
