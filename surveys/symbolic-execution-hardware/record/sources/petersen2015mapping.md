# Guidelines for Conducting Systematic Mapping Studies in Software Engineering: An Update

## Evidence

The abstract and method overview describe systematic mapping as a structured
process connecting research questions, search, selection, classification, and
reporting of a research area.

## Bearing on RQs

This is survey-method evidence rather than an answer to RQ1–RQ5. It supports
the use of an explicit classification scheme and a traceable bounded map. It
does not imply that the present search is closed, exhaustive, or a population
estimate.
